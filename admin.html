User Guide

How to post news:

1. Go to the "News Articles" tab.
2. Fill in the Headline, Category, and Short Summary.
3. Tap "Choose File" to pick a picture from your phone gallery.
4. Check "Breaking News" if it's urgent.
5. Write the full story in the big box.
6. Tap "PUBLISH ARTICLE". It will appear on the homepage instantly!

How to delete:

Scroll down to the list and tap the red "Delete" button.
